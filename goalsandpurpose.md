# Project Goals and Purpose

## 1. Main goals of this project

- To explain poverty in the **contemporary world** in a way that goes beyond the common globalization framework, showing how poverty is shaped by global interdependence, history, politics, and power.
- To help students understand how local poverty is connected to decisions and structures in other countries, international organizations, and global markets.
- To introduce key **actors** (governments, international organizations, NGOs, businesses, communities) and their roles in causing, sustaining, or reducing poverty.

## 2. Purpose of this Markdown file

- To serve as the **core content** for a webpage project on poverty, easily adaptable into platforms like Google Sites, WordPress, or other digital formats.
- To provide a structured, **academic‑style outline** that can guide your group’s research, writing, and presentation for the prelim examination.
- To support **critical thinking** by organizing sections on causes, effects, actors, and solutions, so your group can analyze poverty from multiple perspectives and propose meaningful ways forward.
