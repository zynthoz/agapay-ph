About Agapay

Research for dignified work and resilient livelihoods

Agapay is a public-interest knowledge platform focused on informal labor, social protection, and structural poverty in the Philippines. The site synthesizes local and national evidence into practical narratives that support policy dialogue, civic awareness, and actionable reform.

What this platform does

We translate technical labor and development reports into clear, grounded explainers. Each article bridges raw data with lived realities, so readers can understand not just how many people are employed, but what kind of jobs they have and how secure those jobs really are.

Why this matters

Employment growth can hide worsening precarity. Informal workers often carry high risk and low protection, especially during health, climate, and economic shocks. Agapay centers this gap between employment quantity and employment dignity.

Our evidence approach

The content draws from labor briefers, policy studies, and institutional reporting. We prioritize source transparency, context-rich interpretation, and comparisons across time, sectors, and regions to surface patterns that are usually overlooked.

Who this is for

Students, advocates, policy researchers, local planners, and anyone tracking worker vulnerability and social protection outcomes. The goal is to make complex labor evidence understandable and useful for public conversation.
